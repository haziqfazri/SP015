Learning outcomes — 8.1(a), 8.2(a–c)

Stress and strain retain the tension/compression sign. In the linear elastic region, Young’s modulus is the stress–strain gradient; the triangular areas give elastic strain energy and energy density.

σ = F/A, ε = ΔL/L₀, Y = σ/ε U = ½FΔL, u = ½σε

Learning outcomes — 8.1(b–d)

Elastic deformation is recoverable; plastic deformation is permanent. Ductile materials show much greater elongation before fracture than brittle materials.

Tension is plotted in the positive quadrant and compression in the negative quadrant. The shaded triangle is labelled by its non-negative magnitude because stored elastic energy cannot be negative. Visible deformation is exaggerated.
