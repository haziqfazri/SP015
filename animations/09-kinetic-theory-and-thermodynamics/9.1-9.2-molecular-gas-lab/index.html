Ideal-gas assumptions

Molecules are numerous and negligibly small compared with their spacing. They move randomly, exert no force except during brief collisions, and collide elastically with one another and the walls.

The chamber is a time-scaled 2D projection. The equations and numerical readouts describe the full 3D ideal gas; collision counts do not calculate pressure.

Equipartition model

Each active quadratic degree contributes one half k T per molecule. This model uses f equals 3, 5, or 6 for monatomic, diatomic, and generic polyatomic gases.

Vibrational modes and their temperature dependence are outside the SP015 scope represented here.
