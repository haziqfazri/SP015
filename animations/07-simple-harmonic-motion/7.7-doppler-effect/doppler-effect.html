Learning Outcomes — 7.7(a), 7.7(b)

The Doppler Effect is the change in observed frequency of a wave caused by relative motion between the source and the observer. Limited here to one moving party at a time: a stationary observer with a moving source, or a stationary source with a moving observer.

Key Equations

Moving source, stationary observer:

f' = fv / (v ∓ vₛ)

Moving observer, stationary source:

f' = f(v ± v₀) / v
