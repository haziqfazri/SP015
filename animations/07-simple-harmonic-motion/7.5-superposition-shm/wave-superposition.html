Learning Outcome — 7.5(a)

State the principle of superposition of waves for constructive and destructive interference: when two waves meet, the resultant displacement is the sum of each wave's individual displacement.

y_resultant = y_A + y_B

Learning Outcomes — 7.5(a), 7.5(c)

Two identical progressive waves, offset by a phase difference, combine by the same superposition rule as the pulse mode — but because these waves never end, the interference pattern is continuous rather than a single momentary overlap.

y_resultant = y_A + y_B

What to notice

Each pulse keeps its own shape and amplitude before and after overlap — superposition is a momentary addition, not a permanent change to either wave.
