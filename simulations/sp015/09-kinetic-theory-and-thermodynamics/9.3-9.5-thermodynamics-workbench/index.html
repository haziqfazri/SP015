Learning outcome — 9.3

State and apply the First Law of Thermodynamics to account for heat transferred to a gas and work done by the gas.

ΔU = Q − W

Sign convention

Q > 0 means heat enters the gas. W > 0 means work is done by the gas on the surroundings.

The ledger connects two states without claiming an isothermal, isobaric, isochoric, or adiabatic path.
