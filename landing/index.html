Why this exists

Physics is a relationship,
not a picture to memorize.

These simulations turn the curriculum into a set of controllable experiments. Adjust a launch angle, pull a spring, move a source, or tune a string — then read the numbers alongside the motion.

The goal is not to replace problem solving. It is to give students a place to build an intuition for the variables before they solve for them on paper.
